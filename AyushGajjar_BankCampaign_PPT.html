Key EDA Insights

  • Near-balanced target — ~47% yes vs ~53% no
  • Age: Bell-shaped, majority 25–55 years
  • Balance: Right-skewed, most ₹0–₹2,000
  • Duration: Strongest correlation with subscription
  • Job Type: Management & retired convert most
  • Education: Tertiary-educated respond better

Critical Finding

Subscribers had significantly longer average call durations — suggesting engagement level is a key predictor.

Correlation Insight

duration has the highest positive correlation with the target variable among all features.

Business Recommendations

🎯

Target High-Value Customers

Prioritise customers with higher account balances — they are significantly more likely to convert.

🔄

Re-Target Past Successes

Customers with prior positive campaign outcomes (poutcome_success) show dramatically higher conversion.

⏱️

Optimise Call Strategy

Longer, quality conversations drive subscriptions. Train agents to engage, not rush.

Projected Impact

Implementing ML-driven lead scoring can reduce call-center costs by up to 40% while improving campaign conversion rates through targeted outreach.
